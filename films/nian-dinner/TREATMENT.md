# 《年兽来吃年夜饭》 — Treatment

Style: Red Paper-cut (`styles/papercut-red`). 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 144 s. All on-screen text in Simplified Chinese. No voice-over: the storyteller's few lines are paper banners (横批 strips) in the world, exported as `.srt`.

## 0. Brief, as decided (nobody to ask)

- Topic, length, style, language, sound and the producer notes: `CLOUD-BRIEF.md`, taken whole.
- Cast: **小雪** (the girl, about seven), **奶奶** (grandmother, with whom she lives), **年兽** (the Nian), **爸爸妈妈** (on the bus), two **villagers** with a firecracker pole, the **bus driver** (seen only as a cap in the window).
- Names of the village and the bus company: none (no real brands, DIRECTOR.md §12).
- Music: original score from the cue map, pentatonic D-gong mode, in the style's palette (erhu, dizi → `flute`, suona → own synthesis, zheng → `dan_tranh`, pipa → `pluck`, woodblock, frame drum, bass drum, cymbals, small and big gong). Foley synthesised from `window.EV`.
- Storyboard review: no. Own material: none.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. One night, one clock (chosen).** Linear, from dusk to midnight, with the clock as the ticking ruler: 10:00 → 11:30 → 11:55 → 12:00. The paper-cut 小雪 is cutting grows with the night and is the measure of time on screen. The third act cross-cuts the stuck bus with the village. Why: the topic already has a deadline (midnight) and a thing being made (the paper-cut); the clock gives the comedy a floor and the rescue a ceiling.

**B. Through one window.** The whole film seen from or through the same window: the courtyard beyond it, the paper-cuts pasted on it, the Nian's face appearing in it, the bus headlights through it, and at the end the window lit from inside. Elegant, but the rescue on the mountain road would have to be seen from far away, and the brief wants the bus shot properly.

**C. Two roads to the gate.** Parallel montage from the first frame: the bus leaving the city on one road, the Nian coming down the mountain on the other, the village between them; they meet at the gate at midnight. Strong shape, but it spends the first minute on travel and leaves no room for the comedy of the Nian and the girl getting to know each other, which is the heart of the brief.

Chosen: **A**, with B's device (the paper-cut taking shape, the window pay-off) and C's cross-cut only in the last act.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline.** On New Year's Eve, a girl whose parents are stuck on the mountain road feeds the lonely Nian a bowl of dumplings; the beast that everyone scares away carries her parents' bus home before midnight, and earns a seat at the table and a place on the window.

- **Setup.** A snowy village pastes red paper-cuts and hangs firecrackers to keep the Nian away. 小雪 and 奶奶 fold dumplings; two chairs stay empty. 小雪 cuts a folded sheet, a shape nobody can read yet. On the mountain the Nian is not hungry for people, only alone; the smell of dumplings drifts up.
- **Turn.** 小雪 leaves a bowl outside the gate. The Nian comes, sneezing at every window flower and diving into a snowdrift at the first firecracker; she is the only one not afraid. They fold dumplings together (its paws squash them). Then the bus: stuck in a drift on the mountain, headlights blinking far away. The Nian understands, runs up the mountain and carries the bus down on its back. At the gate the villagers light the firecrackers; the Nian freezes.
- **Ending.** Silence. 小雪 steps between the fuse and the beast and unfolds the paper-cut she has been cutting all night: the Nian's own portrait. The villagers put out the fuse; the bus door opens; parents; midnight gong. At the table the Nian is squeezed in with the giant dumpling it made; she pastes the Nian paper-cut on the window; from outside, every window glows, hers with a beast among the flowers, and the snow that falls is the scraps from her scissors.

## 3. Benchmark

- **The 1958–59 Shanghai cut-paper shorts** (grammar only): profile acting with a jointed rig, poses held and popped, three or four paper layers in parallax, a fully front-lit paper world. Learn the staging (one figure, one action, a clean silhouette against a plain paper ground) and the deadpan comic timing. Do not take any character, prop or scene.
- **"Bao" (Pixar, 2018)**: a wordless food-and-family short. Learn: how a dumpling, folded on screen, becomes a feeling; the table as the final image; how a comic middle turns tender without a line of dialogue; the close-up held before the pay-off. Do not take the dumpling character, the kitchen, the mother, or any shot.
- Not taken from either: melodies, type, character designs.

## 4. Look

- **Paper families.** Night papers: far hills in a lighter dusty indigo, near layers in a deeper indigo; snow is an off-white rice paper with sawtooth drifts; the sky a dyed indigo with a grey-violet band at dusk that goes out by act C. Story pieces are China red (`#d2201f`); the Nian one step deeper (`#b0141c`, the heavy one); 小雪 one step brighter (`#e0352c`); grandma and the villagers plain red; far-off window flowers pale red. Warm lantern pools in act D and after. Gold foil once: the midnight gong and the clock's hands at twelve.
- **Light tells the time.** Act A–B: dusk (sky band, long pale ground, windows unlit). Act C–G: night (sky dark, lightmap with lantern pools and the bus's two headlights as emissive pieces). Act H–I: midnight backlight, the one time the paper glows: every window flower emissive, holes at full light, bloom, rays on the big window.
- **Figures.** Red block + inset contour (double outline) on every piece; eyes = two crescents and a pupil hole; swappable face pieces, never morphs. Fur, collars, tiles and drifts are sawtooth rows; scales and quilting crescents; joints swirls. No ring cut without a bridge.
- **The Nian.** A big round body (height 2.6 × the girl), sawtooth fur all round, two curled horns with crescent cuts, round eyes with large pupil holes so the eye acting reads at wide shots, a wide mouth with a sawtooth tooth row, short legs, oversized paws with three claw slits, a tufted tail. Face pieces: lonely, sniff, sneeze (eyes shut, mouth wide), panic (pupils tiny, brows up and apart), happy, eat (tongue), determined (brows down and in), soft (lids half down).
- **小雪.** Round head, two braids ending in pom-poms (the pom-poms bounce on every step), padded jacket with a fur collar, quilt crescents, boots. Face pieces: neutral, smile, surprise, sad, determined, laugh (eyes closed arcs), worried.
- **Sets.** Village of five houses stepped up a slope, tiled roofs, red couplets at every door, window lattices with rosettes, a gate tower (门楼) with a lantern on each side, one bare persimmon tree with cut-branch sawtooth, a stone wall. Mountain road: three parallax ridges, a zigzag road cut into the near ridge, bare pines (triangle sawtooth), the bus. Kitchen table top-down: a round board, flour as a pale paper layer, a bowl, dumpling pleats cut as crescents.
- **Type.** Title: cut letters (yang-cut, counters are holes) from a CJK font subset, popping in character by character on 12 fps steps, with sawtooth border strips and a code-cut seal. Banners: a red strip darker than the picture (`#8f1016`), swallow-tail ends, serrated edge, cream text, Noto Serif SC 700 at 44 px; drops in on two 12 fps steps. End card the same.

## 10. Not the demo again

Checked after writing this: the demo (`DEMO.md`, read after this treatment) must differ in at least four of: structure, opening, signature shot, camera path, score shape, ending. Ours: structure = one night on a clock with a rescue; opening = ECU scissors → scrap becomes snow; signature = the Nian carrying the bus downhill with headlights lighting the snow; camera path = roof view planted and returned to, fast parallax track on the run; score = lonely erhu → playful suona → action → silence → gong → finale; ending = backlit window with the beast among the flowers and the scraps as snow.
