# 《最后一班渡船》 — Treatment

Style: `styles/hd-2d` (pixel sprites in a lit 3D diorama, high three-quarter camera, tilt-shift band, practicals with shadows, RPG UI). The term "HD-2D" never appears in the film.
Language of the film: Simplified Chinese (title, chapter cards, dialogue boxes, `.srt`). No voice-over, no spoken dialogue.
Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 146 s. Mix −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, grain 0.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. A day-cycle map.** One set (the river crossing of a Jiangnan water town) seen from dawn to dawn; five chapters (清晨 正午 黄昏 夜 黎明), each hour lit by different practicals as the ferry's last day unfolds. The storm crossing is the night chapter. Strength: the one set makes every light change legible (the same walls, the same water, a new light), and the bridge beside the ferry stays in every wide as the clock. Weakness: the obvious shape for a "last day" story; it needs a strong hook and a real pay-off to not feel like a schedule.

**B. Forty crossings.** The UI counts crossings like a save file (第 14,601 次 …); each chapter is one crossing; the counter stops at the night crossing, which is "one more" than the ferry was supposed to make. Strength: the RPG grammar does the storytelling. Weakness: numbers on screen fight the quiet tone, and the counter is a gimmick the audience has to decode.

**C. The paper boat's film.** Told from the water: every scene begins and ends on the surface of the river, where Xiao Shi's paper boats pass; the storm is seen from below the pier; the last shot is the boat under the bridge. Strength: a single native object carries set-up and pay-off. Weakness: the water-level camera is not the style's camera; the diorama needs its high view to read as a diorama.

**Choice: A, with C's opening and ending folded in.** The film is one set lit five ways, told through the ferry's crossings, but it opens on the water with a paper boat being set down (the set-up, inside the first three seconds) and ends on the water with the last paper boat drifting under the new bridge (the pay-off). The high camera owns the chapters; the water-level camera is kept for the two bookends and the three closest beats of the storm, so when it comes down it means something.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline.** On the last day before the new bridge opens, an old ferryman makes his final crossings and, in the night storm that follows, one more that no bridge could have made.

- **Set-up.** Morning. Lao Zhou rows the schoolchildren across; Xiao Shi, at the bow, drops a paper boat on the water as he does every day. Beside the ferry stands the new bridge, fenced, with its middle span still missing. Tomorrow it opens; today the ferry closes after forty years. Noon brings a wedding party under red umbrellas; dusk brings an old couple with a goose, and Lao Zhou lights his lantern for what he calls the last crossing.
- **Turn.** Night. He ties up, blows out the lantern and looks at the dark bridge. A storm breaks. Xiao Shi's mother runs to the pier with the boy burning with fever; the clinic is across the river and the bridge cannot be crossed. Lao Zhou relights the lantern and rows them over through the storm; the lantern nearly dies mid-river and he shields it with his body; the clinic door opens in warm light.
- **Ending.** Dawn. The bridge opens with firecrackers and a red ribbon, and the whole town crosses it. Lao Zhou sits alone in his moored boat. Xiao Shi, well again, runs down to the old pier with a paper boat that says 谢谢周爷爷. Lao Zhou sets it on the water and it drifts under the new bridge into the morning light.

**Set-ups and pay-offs.**
| Planted | Paid off |
|---|---|
| Xiao Shi's paper boat on the water (0:03, and again in the morning wide) | The written paper boat at dawn; the last shot |

## 3. Benchmark

- *Octopath Traveler II* (2023), the grammar the style names: how a high three-quarter wide holds one tiny lit subject inside a tilt-shift band; how a lamp's shadow swings; how the dialogue box sits in the frame. **Learn:** the camera heights, the bloom on practicals, the dialogue-box rhythm. **Don't take:** characters, places, UI ornaments, music, names.
- *Spirited Away* (2001), the ferry at dusk and the lamps on the water: how windows light up in a cadence across a waterfront, how a boat's single lamp reads against a dark river. **Learn:** the dusk colour logic (violet sky, warm windows), the lights-popping-on cadence. **Don't take:** designs, characters, the melody.
- *The Old Man and the Sea* (Petrov, 1999): hands and a face in a struggle on water; the weather as the antagonist; patience with a close-up. **Learn:** close-ups of hands on the oar, holding a face while the weather does the acting, letting the climax slow down. **Don't take:** images, the painterly technique, the story.

## 4. The world

**Set:** one diorama, a river bend in a Jiangnan water town in autumn. The near (south) bank is the town: whitewashed walls, black tile roofs, a stone lane along the water, the old stone pier with steps and a wooden jetty, a lantern post, a small roofed shelter, a 乌桕 tree in red-orange leaf and willows. The far (north) bank is quieter: a few houses, the clinic (whitewashed, a door and one window, a small sign 卫生所), stone steps up from the water, a lane to the school. The river is wide enough to need a ferry (about 28 m). To the east, parallel to the ferry line, the **new bridge**: concrete piers and beam spans, the middle span missing until dawn, red-and-white barrier fence, a small crane on the far side, one red construction lamp that blinks at night.

**The ferry:** a flat wooden boat with a low arched canopy amidships, a stern oar (摇橹) worked standing, a lantern hung on a short pole at the stern beside Lao Zhou. The paper boats are white with a fold line, 4–5 px long in the wide, filling a quarter of the frame in the inserts.

**Characters** (pixel sprites ~48 px tall at the style's density; portraits at 48×48 px drawn by hand for the dialogue boxes):
- **老周 Lao Zhou**, 68, the ferryman. Indigo cloth jacket (the one saturated costume colour no set uses), grey trousers rolled up, straw sandals, a flat straw hat, a white towel on his shoulder, white stubble, deep eye folds. Expressions: calm, smile (eyes crinkled), grave (brows down and in), resolved (jaw set, brows level), fear (eyes wide in the dark), tenderness (soft smile, brows up). Key poses: rowing (push, pull), tying up, sitting with hat on knees, striking a match, blowing out, cupping the lantern, kneeling at the water.
- **小石 Xiao Shi**, 8. Yellow school cap (the findable colour), blue-white school tracksuit, red scarf, a satchel. Expressions: curious (brows up, mouth small o), happy (closed-eye smile), fevered (eyes shut, cheeks flushed, mouth open), shy-proud (eyes up, cheeks pink). Poses: sitting at the bow, leaning over to drop the boat, waving, running, holding out the boat with both hands.
- **母亲 the mother**, plum jacket, hair tied, carrying the boy in a grey blanket. Expressions: frantic (brows up and in, mouth open), relief. Poses: running with the child, pleading, climbing the steps.
- **新娘 the bride** in red with a red veil-cloth, the groom in red with a hat, four guests with red umbrellas, one with a drum.
- **老伯 the old man** in brown, his wife in grey with a basket, and a **white goose**.
- A nurse in white at the clinic door; the crowd at dawn (the same sprites plus a few townspeople).

## 9. Type and UI

- **Chapter cards** and the **title**: Noto Serif SC, weight 600, wide tracking (0.4 em), paper white on warm ink with a thin copper rule under the characters; the title 最后一班渡船 at 96 px with a small copper ornament, the chapter cards at 72 px. Cinzel is kept for nothing but a small Latin tracking of the date-less "I–V" chapter numerals above the characters (一 二 三 四 五 are better: use the Chinese numerals in Noto Serif SC instead; no Latin on the cards).
- **Dialogue boxes**: the style's box: a dark translucent plate (warm ink at 82 %), a 2 px copper border with corner ornaments, a name plate on the top-left edge (copper plate, ink text), a 48×48 pixel portrait at 4× on the left with a 1 px copper frame, the line in Noto Serif SC 44 px paper white typed at ~28 characters/s then held, a blinking caret, a soft chime on entry. Box width 1280 px, anchored bottom-centre at y = 1080 − 72, never over a face (faces in close-ups are framed in the upper two-thirds).
- **Subtitles**: none burned (no narration). The `.srt` carries the dialogue lines with the box timings.

## 10. Not the demo again

Compared with the library's demo (*The Lampbearer*) after reading `DEMO.md`:

| | The demo | This film |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | a quest: a light handed over, carried through two biomes to a landmark | a day-cycle in one set, five chapters by hour, the crossings as episodes, a storm as the turn |
| Opening | a chapter card, then a wide where the landmark light fails | a water-level insert: a paper boat set down, then a crane up to the dawn wide and the title |
| Signature shot | the lamp room ignition, a rising pull-back | the very high wide of one lantern in the black storm river, then the heartbeat close-up |
| Camera path | harbour → forest lateral track → cliff dive → lamp room → harbour mirror | a single diorama; rack focus to the bridge, top-down, through-the-piers tracking, pier close-ups, a water-level ending |
| Score | a licensed track cut inside at downbeats | an original score on a beat grid in folk instruments, five moods, two silences, a heartbeat |
| Ending | the opening set transformed, title and tagline | the paper boat drifting under the new bridge at dawn, the band narrowing; no title at the end |

Six of six differ. What was learnt from the demo code: the pixel pipeline (ramps, Bayer dither, selout outline), lit billboards with cut-out shadows, the light-0.45-m-toward-the-lens rule for a carried lantern, the flash curve, the tilt-shift folded into the DOF's signed circle of confusion, the sea shader's light columns.

## 11. Production notes

- No GPU: three.js runs on SwiftShader in headless Chrome. Supersampling 2× is the style's post chain; if a frame takes more than ~6 s the render drops to 1.5× and the DOF kernel is trimmed, never the shadows or the lantern.
- Sprites step on twos (12 fps) and walk cycles at 8 fps; camera, light, rain, flashes and focus move every frame.
- Score: `core/audio/sampler.py` (flute as dizi, erhu, dan_tranh as guzheng, violins, violas, cellos, contrabass, timpani, gran_cassa, bass_drum, frame_drum, glockenspiel, hand_chimes, violins_pizz, cellos_spic, violins_trem) and `core/audio/pluck.py` (pipa with lunzhi). Libraries: vsco2ce, vcsl, karoryfer (all CC0).
- Foley: `core/audio/sfx.py` driven by `window.EV` exported by `events.mjs`; rain, wind and thunder synthesised from filtered noise; the goose from a pulsed, formant-filtered saw; firecrackers from dense click bursts.
- Loudness −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (mux.sh targets −1.2 dBTP), grain 0.
