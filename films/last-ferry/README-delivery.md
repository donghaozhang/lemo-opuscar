# 《最后一班渡船》 The Last Ferry

A 150-second film in the library's pixel-diorama style (pixel sprites in a lit 3D model town, high three-quarter camera, tilt-shift band, practical lights with shadows, chapter cards and dialogue boxes). On the last day before the new bridge opens, the old ferryman 老周 makes his final crossings through a Jiangnan water town in autumn: the schoolchildren at dawn (小石 drops a paper boat on the water, as he does every day), a wedding party under red umbrellas at noon, an old couple with a goose at dusk, when he lights his lantern for what he calls the last crossing. At night he blows the lantern out; a storm breaks; 小石's mother runs to the pier with the boy burning with fever, the clinic is across the river and the bridge still has a span missing, so 老周 relights the lantern and rows them through the storm, the lantern nearly dying mid-river. The clinic door opens in warm light. At dawn the bridge opens with firecrackers and a red ribbon and the whole town crosses it; 老周 sits alone in his boat until 小石, well again, brings him a paper boat that says 谢谢周爷爷, and he sets it on the water, where it drifts under the new bridge into the morning light.

**How it was made.** Everything is code: a three.js diorama of primitives wrapped in procedurally drawn pixel textures, pixel characters drawn per pixel from pose tables at two densities (wide shots and close-ups, so eyes, lids and brows read), a custom physical depth-of-field with the tilt-shift folded into it, bloom and a filmic grade, rendered frame by frame in headless Chrome on software OpenGL (no GPU) at 1920×1080, 24 fps. One timeline file carries the shots, the beat grid, every hit and the dialogue; the original score (dizi, erhu, guzheng, pipa, strings, timpani, frame drum) was written on that grid with the library's sampler and physical-model plucked strings, the foley and ambience were synthesized on the page's own event list, and the mix was mastered to −14 LUFS. Fonts: Noto Serif SC (subset), Cinzel (OFL). Full credits in `CREDITS` of the source folder.
