# TREATMENT v2 — 《深夜翻译官》 The Night Translator

1920s Silent Film · ≈ 145 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · Simplified Chinese intertitles · no voice
Brief: `CLOUD-BRIEF-V2.md` (a substantially better v2). v1's treatment is kept as `TREATMENT-v1.md`.

## 0. Decisions taken without the owner

- **Length 145 s** (brief: 100–150). 18 music sections, ~70 shots, average shot ≈ 2 s, from 0.7 s (montage) to 9 s (the
  catch, the dawn pull-back).
- **Sound vs. the style**: as in v1, "Foley" is the picture-house way: the pianist plus the effects drummer's trap box.
  New in v2: a **small photoplay orchestra** of the period (upright piano, violins, cellos, clarinet, bassoon, flute, muted
  trumpet, timpani — VSCO 2 CE, CC0), as a 1920s picture-house pit band would have it. Still no recorded room sound.
- **Period tinting per sequence** (`STYLE.md` §3 allows it "as a valid alternative to sepia; one tint per section"):
  - night in the archive (cold open, establishing, the three reels at the desk): **blue** night tint;
  - the films-within-the-film in the viewer, each reel its own period tint: **western amber**, **romance rose**,
    **monster green** — the audience reads the genre before the card;
  - cards: neutral silver; the montage: blue thinning to neutral as the hours pass;
  - **the fire: no tint** — harsh, cold, untinted silver with heavy damage and the burn. A red fire tint was period-
    correct but would kill the one colour; the red ink must stay the only red;
  - tender beat: warm sepia; dawn: **amber-gold**, the warmest and cleanest print.
- **The one colour is still the red ink**, and in v2 it travels: it belongs to 老校 (corrections, the pen nib); in the
  tender beat 老校 hands the red pen to 小译; at dawn 小译 stamps the red seal 「译毕」 itself.
- **Fonts** (all OFL): Noto Serif SC (cards), **Ma Shan Zheng** brush script for the red handwritten corrections,
  Playfair Display SC / Old Standard TT (studio cards), and one period display face per genre reel card: **Rye**
  (western wood type), **Pinyon Script** (romance), **UnifrakturMaguntia** (expressionist monster picture).
- **Closing**: the credits go on the end card ("翻译 小译 · 校对 老校") instead of a separate card, to stay under 150 s.

## 1. Three candidate structures (v2)

1. **The rule of three, then the fire** (chosen). Cold open → title → the boss sets the rule → three reels, each a
   faster variation of the same gag (literal translation → red correction → a thrown page), the third subverted →
   montage → the jam and the fire → teamwork → dawn. Comedy needs a pattern before it can break one; the three genres
   give variety on top of the pattern.
2. **One reel, many shots.** v1's structure with more coverage. Better looking, but not deeper; rejected.
3. **Two nights.** A failed first night (the boss rejects everything), a better second. Too long for 150 s; rejected.

## 2. Logline and arc

A small tin translator must turn a thousand English-captioned reels into Chinese before dawn under a fussy proofreader's
red pen — and when the last reel catches fire, the two of them become a team.

- **Cold open (hook):** in the dark, a film can rolls across the floor and bumps a sleeping robot; its antenna bulb
  flickers on; it looks up — a mountain of cans to the ceiling; double take.
- **Set-ups:** the rule (「天亮之前——一个字也不许错。」); the clock at midnight; the fire bucket 消防 on the far wall;
  one empty slot on the shelf in a moonbeam; 老校's throwing arm; 小译's antenna bulb lights when it is happy.
- **Development:** three reels — western ("HOLD YOUR HORSES!" → 「抓住你的马！」 → 「别急！」, the page bonks 小译),
  romance ("I'M HEAD OVER HEELS!" → 「我头在脚跟上！」 — 小译 tries it, standing on its head → 「我神魂颠倒！」, 小译 ducks
  the page), monster ("IT'S RAINING CATS AND DOGS!" → 小译 imagines it → 「天上下猫下狗！」 → 「倾盆大雨！」, 小译 **catches**
  the thrown page one-handed, reads it, nods; 老校's monocle pops).
- **Montage:** the night's work, the clock spinning, the mountain shrinking (match cut clock face → reel).
- **Turn (the set piece):** the last can, No. 1000, jams; the swinging lamp; smoke; 「硝酸胶片——一点就着！」; hot-potato
  juggling; the race along the shelves (a ladder near-miss) cross-cut with 老校 fetching the bucket; the trip; the can
  in the air in silence while our own print burns; the catch (pay-off: the throwing arm catches).
- **Tender:** the soaked film hung to dry between them like laundry; 老校 hands over the red pen; 小译 writes the last
  line; the raised arm (flinch) becomes a pat; dialogue card 「……不错。」
- **Dawn:** 小译 stamps the red seal 「译毕」; 老校 lifts it to the empty slot (pay-off); the lamps come on row by row; the
  pull-back reveals the lit archive; 「您刚看的这一卷——正是第一千卷。」; 完, with the credits 翻译 小译 · 校对 老校.

## 3. Benchmark

## 4. Characters

- **小译 Xiao Yi** (theme: a bouncy clarinet tune in C over stride piano). Film-can drum on one wheel. Acting tools: the
  wheel (speed, skid), the springy antenna and its bulb (follow-through; lit = happy), two lamp eyes with lids and brow
  plates, squash on landings, a crouch before every effort, idle breathing (1.6 s cycle).
- **老校 Lao Jiao** (theme: a pompous dotted march for bassoon and cellos in G minor → G major at dawn). Tall iron
  hat-stand on three casters, a square head with a hinged monocle lens (it telescopes out to read and pops on surprise,
  with a spring wobble), a red-nibbed pen arm (points, flourishes, strikes, scribbles), a stamp arm. Slow sway as idle.
