# 《蒸笼爵士》 — Treatment

1930s Rubber Hose Cartoon · 1920×1080, 4:3 gate · 24 fps · ≈ 140 s · Simplified Chinese on screen · no voice, no vocals.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A night shift in three numbers (the backstage musical).** Sleepy overture → the big kitchen number → the chase → a solo → the finale. The kitchen is a stage, the tools are the band, the sleeping baker is the audience who never sees the show. The music *is* the plot: every act changes tempo, and the emotional beat is the moment the band stops.
2. **The relay of a bun.** One bun passed hand to hand (rolling pin → steamer → rat → snow → newsboy → back to the baker), each hand-off a new instrument on the melody. Elegant, but the kitchen number and the chase become a chain of small passes and lose the "whole world dances" peak the brief wants.
3. **The clock's story.** Told from the stopped alarm clock's point of view: frozen at 4:00, it watches the night and only starts again when someone does an honest thing. A strong frame, but the clock can't move, so the comedy would have to be watched from a shelf.

**Chosen: 1**, borrowing from 3: the stopped clock is the frame — it opens the film (insert, 4:00, the key unturned) and its winding is the pay-off that brings the morning. The tempo map follows the acts: sleepy 72 → hot 120 → chase 144 → lone clarinet (free) → stomp 144.

- **Opening:** a title card rolls up like a window shade on the beat, then an **iris opens on a falling snowflake** that lands on the 「王记包子」 sign — the lane at 4 a.m.
- **Ending:** the queue in the morning lane, then the final gag (the rat in an apron kneading on the beat); the rat grabs the iris and pulls it shut; 「完」.

## 2. Logline and arc

While old Baker Wang sleeps through 4 a.m., his kitchen tools play hot jazz and make the morning's buns; when a rat's raid spills them into the snow, a hungry newsboy catches every one — and gives them all back, so the kitchen gives him the best bun and winds the clock that was never set.

- **Setup (0–30 s):** title; iris on the snowy lane; the shop sign and the door card 「六点开门」; inside, Wang kneads, yawns, and — insert — the alarm clock reads 4:00, its key unwound; his hand passes it. He nods off into the dough. Snore. The kettle opens one eye.
- **Act 2a (30–64 s): the kitchen number.** The cleaver counts in on the board; the band hits; rolling pin, cleaver, kettle, flame and the concertina steamer stack make buns as a dance. Outside the frosted window a ragged newsboy rubs a peephole and watches, stomach growling (planted twice).
- **Act 2b (64–96 s): the rat and the chase.** A rat tiptoes in on woodblock ticks, grabs a bun; the kettle sees — band stop — chase at 144: up the steamer stack, the stack sways like a concertina and topples, the door bursts, buns roll into the snowy lane; tilt with the fall, trucking pan along the lane; the newsboy's rubber-hose arms stretch and catch each bun on a xylophone note. The rat skids on ice and is gone.
- **Turn (96–110 s):** the lane is silent but for snow. Held close-up: the newsboy, arms full of buns, the last warm one steaming under his nose. The band drops to a lone clarinet. Glances on woodblock ticks (shop door / bun / shop door). Stomach growl. He smiles, walks to the door and knocks.
- **Pay-off (110–128 s):** he hands every bun back through the door. The kettle sheds a tear of steam and slips him the best bun; the tools look at the clock — still 4:00 — the rolling pin and cleaver wind the key; the hands spin; the window greys to dawn (the whole frame changes); 6:00: RING. Wang wakes with a big take, sees perfect buns and the honest boy at the door, gives him a second bun and pats his cap.
- **Ending (128–140 s):** the boy runs into the morning lane waving a newspaper: insert — the headline prints itself 「王记包子 开门啦！」 — and a queue forms, swinging on the beat (pull-back to a packed medium-full). Final gag: the rat in an apron kneading in time; it winks, grabs the iris and pulls it shut. 「完」.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Early-1930s sound cartoons where a kitchen or a town comes alive at night** — *learn:* the whole set breathing on the beat, Mickey-moused props, the stop before each gag, the iris as a physical object, staging every gag in a medium so the faces read. *Don't take:* any character, design, song, gag or title.
- **Cuphead (2017)** — *learn:* how a modern team finishes the look (ink weight, film damage, big-band orchestration with clear solos). *Don't take:* characters, bosses, melodies.
- **Ratatouille's "food made with joy" sequences** — *learn:* the kitchen as a choreographed workplace, ingredients as a rhythm section. *Don't take:* designs or shots.

## 4. Look

- **Grey ramp:** cool silver-nitrate greys (blue-free, neutral-cool) for night; at dawn the whole frame lifts one step and the window turns to the brightest background grey. Characters own pure white and ink.
- **Kitchen set (a stage):** checker floor, tiled wall with a pattern band, the stove with a round firebox door, a shelf with the alarm clock, the frosted window upper right, the door on the left with the card 「六点开门」, a rat hole in the skirting board lower right, the dough table centre.
- **Lane (弄堂):** stone-gate (石库门) door frames, brick walls, laundry poles, a lamppost, snow on everything, footprints.
- **Light tells the time:** 4 a.m. = dark lane, one lamppost and the warm-grey glow from the shop window; inside, the stove and an oil lamp are the practicals (a radial lift around each). 6 a.m. = the whole frame (walls, floor, figures) lifts and the window turns bright.

## 9. Type

- Title: **ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou** (OFL; 1930s Shanghai poster geometry) as the fat display face: white face, heavy ink outline, ink drop shadow; characters hop on the beat.
- Title cards / subtitles: **ZCOOL XiaoWei** (OFL; old-style Song) in paper white on a near-black plate with a double-rule border and dot-and-ring corners, drawn in the scene layer so it weaves and flickers with the film; pops on/off. Shop sign and door card are painted in the world.
- `.srt`: the title, the place card, the door card, the clock times (4:00 / 6:00 inserts as cards), the headline and 「完」.

## 10. Native moves

Everything breathes on the beat (whole kitchen at the peak, background squash ×3); Mickey-mousing (each prop an instrument); rubber-hose stretch (the newsboy's catch); transformation (the dough rope → a trombone slide; the clock hands → a spinning top); the film is an object (title shade, the rat pulls the iris shut).
