# 《中秋的灯》 (The Mid-Autumn Lantern) — treatment

Style: Paper-cut Lightbox (`styles/paper-lantern/STYLE.md`, every invariant kept). 1920×1080, 24 fps, 141.5 s.
Language on screen: Simplified Chinese (Ma Shan Zheng for the title and paper strips, Noto Serif SC for subtitles). No voice-over, no dialogue: the story reads from picture, music, sound and a few paper strips; the only subtitles are the radio's garbled words and one closing line.

Written before opening `DEMO.md` or `demo/`. The "Not the demo" section at the end was filled after reading them.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. One straight night (chronological).** Afternoon cutting → dusk → fog → lighthouse → chain of light → reunion, bookended by the real lamp on a table. Plain and strong: every set-up is planted in daylight and paid off at night. Risk: a middle that sags if the fog act is only "things get worse".

**B. The keepsake remembers (frame story).** Open on the lit lamp in a dark room, push into the box, tell the night, pull out: the last pull-out reveals an old, faded rabbit lantern next to the lamp, so the lamp is A-Yue's own memory decades later. Same chronology inside; the frame gives the ending a second meaning without a word.

**C. Two windows, one light (parallel).** Cross-cut from the first minute between the boat at sea and the house on shore, both under one moon; the fog takes the moon from both; the lantern chain becomes the shared light. Strong on theme, but the father's thread needs several sea shots early, which steal time from A-Yue, and the audience must care about her first.

**Chosen: B, with C used only from the fog onward.** The chronology of A stays. The frame of B gives the brief's last shot ("the lit lamp on a table, someone's keepsake") its pay-off: the old rabbit lantern beside the lamp. The parallel cutting of C starts only when the fog comes (father at the wheel, mother on the pier, A-Yue on the cliff), so the first act belongs entirely to A-Yue and the set-ups.

Opening image: a dark room, a paper lightbox switched off; a click, and the lamp turns on. Ending image: the same lamp, lit, with the faded rabbit lantern beside it. Score shape: homely → uneasy → one instrument → swell → gentle.

## 2. Logline and arc

A girl cuts a paper rabbit lantern for her fisherman father's homecoming on Mid-Autumn night; when fog swallows the moon and the harbour, her lantern is far too small to guide him, until the whole village climbs the cliff behind her and turns the dead lighthouse into a chain of light.

- **Set-up (0–35 s):** the lamp turns on; the village, the sea and the dark lighthouse on the headland; A-Yue cuts the rabbit lantern; three mooncakes for three people; the village hangs its festival lanterns; mother and daughter wait on the pier under a rising moon.
- **Turn (35–92 s):** fog rolls in and swallows the boats' lights and the moon; the radio: the boats cannot find the harbour mouth, A-Hai's boat is out there; the mother runs to the pier; A-Yue takes her lantern up the cliff to the dark lighthouse; the tiny red glow climbs the tower and sits in the lamp-room window: nothing. She sits and cries. Silence.
- **Pay-off (92–141 s):** a click below: lights on the path; the villagers have followed with their lanterns and line the path and the gallery; the chain burns through the fog; the father sees the line and steers home; the moon burns through; the leap on the pier; three mooncakes, three hands; the rabbit lantern under the moon; pull out to the lamp on the table, and the faded old rabbit lantern beside it.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Lotte Reiniger's silhouette films** (grammar only): figures jointed at shoulder, elbow, hip and knee, read entirely by silhouette; the head tilt and the hands carry the acting; eyes as cut holes. *Learn:* posture-driven acting, rotation-only animation that still has anticipation and follow-through, how a profile reads emotion. *Don't take:* any character design or scene.
- **Commercial paper-cut shadow-box lamps and layered-paper night scenes** (the medium): the value ladder, the warm backlight bleeding through the pulp, soft shadows from one sheet onto the next. *Learn:* 6–9 sheets, one flat colour each, warm holes. *Don't take:* any specific lamp design.
- **Classic lighthouse-rescue picture books** (grammar only): a small person, a dark tower, a line of lights; a storm told with a few big shapes; the pay-off wide. *Learn:* scale (a tiny figure against a tower, then the tower against the sea), the rhythm of "one light, then many", a warm interior after the cold. *Don't take:* plots, characters, text.

## 4. Characters (cut-paper, jointed silhouettes)

Every figure is a front-layer silhouette: dark paper, so the **cut holes glow** with the backlight (eyes, mouth, lantern). Acting is posture, head tilt and hands; expressions are different cut eye/mouth sheets, swapped on a blink (the lids close, the new cut opens). Rotation only, no squash and stretch.

- **阿月 A-Yue**, about eight, 5 heads tall, two hair buns (丸子头) with a ribbon, a short jacket with wide sleeves, trousers, cloth shoes. Eyes: almond holes with a round paper pupil left inside; separate lid sheets; brow slits. Six expressions, each clearly different (checked on the expression sheet): ① concentration (lids half down, mouth a small tilted 'o', tongue tip), ② delight (eyes wide, mouth a wide open crescent), ③ worry (brows up and in, eyes round, mouth small and down), ④ hope (brows high, eyes wide, mouth a small closed smile), ⑤ despair (eyes shut in downward crescents, brows steep, mouth open and down, cut teardrops), ⑥ tearful joy (eyes shut in upward crescents, mouth the widest smile, tear tracks).
- **Mother**, tall, hair in a low bun with a hairpin, apron, sleeves rolled. Her acting is her hands (on A-Yue's shoulder, clasped at her chest, holding a lantern high).
- **Father 阿海 A-Hai**, broad, a fisherman's cap, a coat with a high collar, at the wheel; a pipe-less, plain silhouette so the cap reads at any size.
- **Villagers** (five silhouettes, reused): an old man with a stick, a boy, a woman with a basket, a young fisherman in a hat, a grandmother; each carries one festival lantern: lotus 莲花, fish 鱼, star 星, carp 鲤, plus plain round lanterns for the rest of the line.
- **The rabbit lantern**: a small red paper rabbit, two long ears (one flops, A-Yue straightens it: the one gag), a candle hole in its belly that glows. The film's single accent colour is its red.

## 11. Decisions made for the owner

- Length 141.5 s (within 100–150).
- The rabbit theme is the one melody; the fog act has no melody, only a drone and the flute.
- No dialogue, no voice; the radio's words are subtitles over crackle (a common film convention for garbled radio).
- The "squash and stretch" the brief allows "where the style allows": the style forbids it, so none. Anticipation and follow-through are done with rotation, timing and overshoot of joints; eye acting with cut eye shapes and lids; the one physical gag is the flopping ear.
- Engine: my own Canvas2D compositor (per-layer offscreen sheets, backlight falloff with pulp mottle, fibre grain, top rim and bottom shade, soft shadow of each sheet on the one behind, depth of field per layer, bloom from an emissive map, vignette, camera micro-drift), because this VM has no GPU and three.js runs on SwiftShader (~6× slower). The look follows STYLE.md §1–3 exactly; the implementation differs. The demo engine (`demo/src/`) is read only for technique after this treatment.
