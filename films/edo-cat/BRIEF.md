# Brief summary — 《江户猫追鱼记》

- Style: Ukiyo-e (`styles/ukiyoe`). Topic: a stray cat steals a fish for a hidden kitten; a black kite snatches it;
  a chase through a series of print views (sudden shower on a bridge, snowy temple, rice fields with Fuji, mountain
  pass) with the fishmonger behind; at the sea the cat rides a great wave on a fishing boat and catches the fish; home
  under the bridge; the fishmonger quietly adds a second fish.
- 1920×1080, 24 fps, target ~138 s (brief: 100–150 s). No voice-over.
- On-screen text, cartouches and `.srt` in Simplified Chinese (CJK font: Shippori Mincho / Yuji Syuku-like OFL brush
  font, subset; checked for missing glyphs).
- Score: shamisen, shakuhachi, koto, taiko / shime-daiko, hyōshigi, small bell — sneaky → chase → awe → quiet.
  Foley: paws, wings, fish, rain, snow, waves, wood. −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP.
- No storyboard stop, no owner material: everything drawn and synthesised in code.
- Environment: ffmpeg 9 static build (imageio-ffmpeg) on PATH ahead of system 6.1; core + music tiers installed.
