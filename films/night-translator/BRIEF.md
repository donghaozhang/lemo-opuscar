Answered in full by `CLOUD-BRIEF.md`; no questions asked.

- **Style:** 1920s Silent Film (`styles/silent-film`), STYLE.md kept whole.
- **Topic:** a small robot translator in a vast film archive at night turns a mountain of English-captioned reels into
  Chinese before dawn; a second robot proof-checks and sends mistakes back; one reel jams and smoulders and must be

  action sounded, projector ambience; −14 LUFS, true-peak limited.
