# 《我的怪兽朋友》 My Monster Friend — treatment

Style: Crayon Picture Book (`styles/crayon-book`). 1920×1080, 24 fps, 140 s. No voice: the story's words are the book's printed words, in
Simplified Chinese, with the `.srt` carrying the same lines. Score in the style's palette (ukulele, clarinet, music box, glockenspiel, toy
piano, recorder, shaker, woodblock). Mix −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Decisions the brief left open are made here.

## 1. Three candidate structures

**A. A flip through eight pages (chosen).** One page per stage of the story, each drawn and coloured while we watch, joined by page turns.
The size rule, the crayon box, the balloon and the words 「你好」 are planted on early pages and paid off on later ones. The two night pages
get the film's one watercolour (ultramarine), so night looks like night and the daytime pages stay bare paper.

**B. One huge page with the camera travelling over it.** The whole story on a single sheet (city at the left, kindergarten at the right,
night as a wash over one corner). Elegant, but a day-night-day-night story needs the paper to change, and the wash cannot be undone.

**C. A colouring-book page.** All outlines there from the first frame, each beat colours one area. Too static for a film about a character
who changes size and climbs off the page.

A wins: page turns give the time jumps (night, next morning, play time, night again) and "drawn live" gives the monster's birth. Opening
image: not the book, not the title: a purple crayon tip in extreme close-up mid-stroke, pulling back to reveal it is colouring a purple
moving truck in a new city (we are already inside the book). Ending: the last page, 「我的朋友」, held, and the purple crayon rolled in and
set down at the page's corner: a bookend with the first image (the pull-out to a book on a desk was dropped: the page stays the stage to
the end, which is the opposite of the demo's ending). Score shape: a lonely clarinet lilt, the music box for the magic, a bouncing ukulele for the
trouble, a hush to one music box winding down for the brave moment, then the theme heard in full for the first time at the balloon and the
two monsters, and a lullaby reprise to close.

## 2. Logline and arc

Too shy to say a word at his new kindergarten, Dou Dou draws a purple monster that comes alive and grows or shrinks with his courage;
when a lonely girl's balloon is caught in a tree, the monster shrinks to a bean on his shoulder until he says one word, 「你好」, and it
grows big enough to free the balloon and make him a friend.

- **Setup**: a new city, a new kindergarten, a boy who cannot speak. At night he draws a monster; it climbs off the page. The rule is shown
  at once: he gasps and it shrinks to a mouse; he giggles and it grows.
- **Turn**: the monster rides to kindergarten in his backpack and makes gentle trouble (eats the crayons, sneezes colour, hides in the
  sandpit). At play time Ni Ni sits alone under a tree with her red balloon stuck in the branches. The monster nudges him; he freezes and it
  shrinks to a bean. He breathes, holds out a purple crayon, and says 「你好」.
- **Ending**: the monster grows huge and lifts him to free the balloon. Ni Ni laughs; the monster burps up the yellow crayon it ate that
  morning; she draws a yellow monster that comes alive too. Two monsters play, two children laugh. That night the purple monster climbs
  back into the book. Last page: two children and two monsters on one page, 「我的朋友」.

**Set-ups and pay-offs**: the size rule (page 3 → the bean on page 6 → huge on page 7); the purple crayon (chosen on page 3 → offered on
page 6 → Ni Ni's box on page 7); the crayon box (insert on page 3 → eaten on page 5 → the yellow crayon burped up on page 7); the red
balloon (a tiny red dot drifting past the truck on page 1 → stuck in the tree on page 6 → freed on page 7, and the ribbons in Ni Ni's
hair are the same red); the backpack (insert on page 4 → the monster sneaks out of it on page 5); "cannot say a word" (page 2) → 「你好」
(page 6) → 「我的朋友」 (page 8); the book on the desk (page 3) → the book closing (page 8).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Raymond Briggs, *The Snowman* (1982)**: learn how a drawn creature and a child share a frame without words, how a still background
  holds living figures, how one tune carries the lyrical passage and the ending is tender rather than triumphant. Take nothing of its
  characters, imagery or song.
- **Pixar, *Monsters, Inc.* (2001), the Boo scenes**: learn the comedy of a big soft creature and a small child, the creature's reactions in
  the eyes and the body, and the gag timing of hiding something large. Take no design, no gag, no music.
- **Shaun Tan's picture books**: learn how a page can be quiet and how a few printed words sit in bare paper. Take no imagery.

## 4. The world, the box of crayons, the characters

**The box (eleven crayons + white)**: indigo line `#2a2f6e` (every outline; never black), grape purple `#8a4bb0` (the monster), lemon
yellow `#f2c74a` (Ni Ni's dress, her monster, the sun), balloon red `#d9403a` (the balloon, Ni Ni's ribbons, the one accent),
sky blue `#5fa7d8`, grass green `#5e9f49`, orange `#ef8a3c` (Dou Dou's shirt), earth brown `#8a5a3a` (hair, tree, desk), sand `#dcc38f`
(sandpit, buildings), pink `#f0a0b0` (cheeks), leaf green `#9bc45a`; white for wax resist. **One watercolour, ultramarine**, for the two
night pages (and nothing else): it is the thing that makes the drawn monster's white-wax eyes shine on page 3, so the magic is literally
the material.

**Pages** (each a 2600×1460 sheet the camera moves over; the page is the stage):
1. The new city: tall gable-fronted buildings in child perspective, a small moving truck, Dou Dou at a window with a box, a tiny red
   balloon drifting past high up, the title lettered in the sky.
2. The kindergarten: a fence, a slide, a tree, children playing, a teacher waving; Dou Dou at the gate, head down.
3. Night, the bedroom: Dou Dou sitting up in bed with the open book on his knees, a ceiling lamp on a cord (the practical light: its cone
   of light keeps the bed and the book as bare paper while the ultramarine wash is night everywhere else), the window with a crescent moon
   and white-wax stars that only appear under the wash, a nightstand with a red ball. At 41 s he paints the night behind his monster: the
   brush passes over the book's page and the white-wax eyes shine.
4. Morning (one sheet two pages wide, no page turn inside it): the front door with the backpack on the step; the street that draws itself
   ahead of him with a low yellow sun; then the classroom: a chalkboard, the long table with children and crayon boxes (a slow truck along
   it), the window to the sandpit.
6. The playground: the big tree at the right, Ni Ni under it, the balloon in the branches, bare paper at the left for the words.
7. The same playground page continued: the huge monster, the balloon freed, the yellow monster, the play.
8. Night again, the bedroom (the same page under its wash; the lamp goes off as the book closes), then the last page: two children, two
   monsters, 「我的朋友」, and the purple crayon set down.

**Dou Dou (豆豆)**: round head about 2.5 heads tall, bowl-cut brown hair, orange shirt, blue shorts, mitten hands, tube limbs, a blue
backpack. **Eyes**: white ovals with indigo pupils, a wax catchlight, lids that can close from the top (sleepy, blink), a lower lid for
squinting (laugh), short brow strokes whose angle carries worry or joy; pink scribbled cheeks. Expressions on the sheet: shy, scared,
giggle, curious, brave, laughing, sleepy, surprised.

**The monster (怪兽)**: a big soft purple pear with two nub horns, huge round eyes (same system, bigger), a wide mouth with two little
teeth, short legs, long soft arms with mitten hands, a tuft on top, small wings-like flaps that are really just ears. Five sizes as the
story needs them: bean, mouse, cat, child and huge. Colouring is heavy pressure zig-zag with a lighter cross-hatch and overshoot, so it
reads as a solid purple creature on bare paper. Squash on landings, stretch on reaches, anticipation before every jump.

**Ni Ni (妮妮)**: two pigtails with red ribbons, yellow dress, same eye system; her monster is a smaller yellow blob with one horn and a
curl. **Teacher and four children**: simpler, half-weight outlines, still.
