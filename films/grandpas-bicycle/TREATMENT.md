# 《外公的单车》 · Grandpa's Bicycle — treatment

Style: Watercolor Brush (`styles/watercolor/STYLE.md`). 1920×1080, 24 fps, ≈ 132 s. Simplified Chinese on screen, no voice-over, original acoustic score, procedural foley. Everything drawn in code.

## 0. Decisions taken for the owner (nobody is watching)

- Length 132 s (brief: 100–150). Frame rate 24 fps (brief) even though the style likes 60: the brush moves slowly, 24 is enough and the render is three times shorter.
- The film is a journal: six pages, one per entry. Page turns are the time cuts, paper mist erases within a page.
- Xiao He's **red** (a vermilion shirt, scarf, coat) is the one accent of the film and recurs on every page. The bicycle is ink black; the bell's highlight is unpainted paper.
- Text on screen: title and the two spoken lines in **Noto Serif SC**; the journal notes in **Long Cang** (OFL handwriting); measured values (`50 m`, `1.2 km`, `12 cm`) in **IBM Plex Mono**. The `.srt` carries the two spoken lines and the journal notes, so the story reads with captions on a phone.
- No storyboard review. No user material.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The journal, in order.** Six pages, one per season/age, each painting itself in, each closed by mist and a page turn. Set-up on page one (he lets go), pay-off on the last (she holds him). Simple, honest, the annotation layer carries time.
2. **Backwards from the last page.** Open on the finished last page (two small figures at the end of the lane, 「骑了 50 米，和那年一样」), un-paint it to blank paper, then live the years forward until the same page paints itself again, now understood. Elegant, but it spends the ending twice and the opening gives away the pay-off before the set-up exists.
3. **One lane, two rides, intercut.** The lane as one long handscroll; the summer ride and the spring ride cut against each other over the same 50 metres, the years as small pinned pages along the wall. Strong signature shot, but intercutting two rides blurs who is holding whom, which is the whole point.

**Chosen: 1**, with the hook and the signature shot of 3 folded in: the film opens on the bell (an insert, a ring in the first second), and both rides are shot as the same lateral dolly along the same painted lane, so the spring ride is a match cut to the summer one, slower. Opening image: a thumb flicks a half-painted bell. Ending: the two of them small at the end of the lane, the brush lifts, the page dries. Score shape: a playful 3/4 for summer, a light 4/4 for the school years, a slower minor-leaning 4/4 for leaving, near silence for winter, a warm 4/4 for spring that thins to one felt piano for the close-up and comes back together for the last page.

## 2. Logline and arc

A girl's watercolour journal follows her grandpa's black bicycle through the years: the summer he taught her to ride by letting go, and the spring she rode him down the same lane and told him she was holding on.

- **Set-up (summer, age 6):** the lane, the old 28-inch bicycle, three falls, a scraped knee, 「我扶着呢」, his hand opening on the saddle, 50 metres alone, him small at the end of the lane, waving.
- **Turn (the years):** she rides to school past him at the corner; she rides to the city for university; the bicycle leans in snow, a walking stick appears beside it, he grows old.
- **Ending (spring):** she comes home, lifts him onto the back seat, rings the bell, rides him under the blossom and says 「外公，我扶着您呢」. Last page: the two of them small at the end of the lane, 「骑了 50 米，和那年一样」.

Set-ups and pay-offs, all planted on page one: the **bell** (rung at 0.9 s, rung again in spring); the **back seat** (labelled on the specimen page, carries her bag in autumn, carries him in spring); the **hand on the saddle** that lets go (in spring his hand on her shoulder, and hers covering it); **「我扶着呢」 → 「外公，我扶着您呢」**; **「骑了 50 米」 → 「骑了 50 米，和那年一样」** with the same measured `50 m` label; **him small and waving at the end of the lane** → the two of them small at the end of the lane; the **walking stick** (winter) that leans on the wall while they ride (spring).

## 3. Benchmark

- A naturalist's field journal page (specimen, pen note, measured value) for how information arrives: the thing first, the note after, the number last. **Learn:** the calm order, generous margins, one hero per page. **Don't take:** any layout or drawing.
- The handscroll panorama (a continuous painted world read by a lateral move) for the two rides. **Learn:** the camera as a reader moving along a scroll; figures small in a big world, then close. **Don't take:** any specific scroll's content.
- Hand-drawn family animation of a quiet rural childhood (the texture of lanes, bicycles and summers in pencil and wash) for performance: anticipation before a wobble, a grandparent's stillness against a child's motion. **Learn:** physical comedy timed to breath, eye acting held longer than feels safe. **Don't take:** characters, designs, shots, music.

## 4. Characters and world

- **小荷 Xiao He**: at 6, pigtails with red bands, red short-sleeve shirt, blue shorts, knees scraped; at 10, bob, red scarf, satchel; at 18, long hair, red backpack, jeans; at 25, hair tied, red coat. Eyes: large almond with a dark pupil and a highlight of paper, clear lids and a brow; expressions painted as wash shapes, no outline.
- **外公 Grandpa**: tall, thin, white vest/shirt, grey trousers, cloth shoes, short grey hair; through the pages his hair whitens, his back bends, his cheeks hollow; a walking stick from winter on. Expressions: amused, concentrated, proud, a tired gentle smile, and on the back seat: eyes closed, then open, then a smile.
- **The bicycle**: a black 28-inch roadster, rod brakes, a sprung saddle, a rear rack (the back seat), a round bell on the right grip. Ink black washes, highlights left as paper.
- **The lane**: a brick-and-plaster wall on the left, a row of trees on the right (a locust in summer, bare in winter, blossoming in spring), a corner with a lamp post at the far end, 50 metres long. Palette per page (4–5 hues each, plus ink):
  - summer: `#e9dcb4` warm light, `#8fae62` leaves, `#4d6b3a` shade, `#b8a37f` wall, accent `#c8442a`;
  - school autumn: `#f0d9a0` morning, `#d4913f` leaves, `#a35b2a` deep, `#c2b08c` wall;
  - leaving autumn: `#dcc9a2` grey-gold, `#b9772f` leaves, `#7b4d26`, `#8d9aa6` rain sky;
  - winter dusk: `#c9d0d8` snow-blue, `#6f7d8c` shadow, `#50606f` wall, `#e8b764` lamp (the one warm);
  - spring: `#e8d2cf` blossom pink, `#a3c08a` new green, `#6c8a5a` shade, `#c7b7a0` wall.

## 9. Type and subtitles

- Title 《外公的单车》 Noto Serif SC 600, 88 px, ink `#2b2420` at 85 %, written in with a left-to-right clip and a soft pencil sound, on the specimen page; it returns as a 28 px stamp on the last page.
- Journal notes: Long Cang, 44 px, ink brown `#4a3a2c`, written left to right over a paper halo, with pen leader lines that grow to the thing they name. Hold ≥ characters ÷ 4.5 + 1.5 s (`readcheck.mjs`).
- Measured labels: IBM Plex Mono 26 px, tracking 0.18 em, the same brown, beside a thin leader with a tick at each end.
- Subtitles (the two spoken lines only): Noto Serif SC 40 px, ink at 78 % over a paper halo, bottom centre, held ≥ 4 s. They are also written into the page by pencil as the note the painter made of what was said.
- `.srt`: the two lines plus the journal notes, same timing as on screen.

## 10. Not the demo

Compared after writing this (DEMO.md read afterwards): different structure (a journal across years, not a single transect), different opening (an insert with a sound, not a map or a wide), different signature shot (a lateral dolly repeated across seasons as a match cut), different camera path (pages and inserts, not one continuous move), different score shape (five moods with two silences), different ending (a small stamp and a dried page). None of the demo's story, props, characters or timeline are used.
