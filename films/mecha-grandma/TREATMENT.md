# TREATMENT — 《机甲外婆》 Mecha Grandma

80s Cel Anime · ≈ 140 s · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · all on-screen text in Simplified Chinese · no voice, no vocals

## 0. Decisions taken without the owner

- **No dialogue, all text diegetic.** The story is told by action, faces and screens: a phone chat, a TV typhoon
  warning, the mech's dashboard, the drone's HUD, floor numbers on the tower, a title card and an end card. Every screen
  text is in Chinese and goes into `subtitles.srt`. The style's subtitle face (a semi-condensed sans) is used for the
  `.srt`-style captions only where a phone message needs to be readable at full frame.
- **Names:** 外婆 (unnamed, as the grandson calls her), the grandson 阿杰, the mech 「大黄」 (stencilled on its
  shoulder: the family nickname for the yellow machine).
- **Accent colours** (`STYLE.md` §3: one accent that pops in every lighting set): the **mech yellow** is the film's
  accent; 外婆's own accent is the **red checked cloth** around the lunchbox, so the box reads at any size.
- **Colour models (色指定) per lighting:** night-neon street (indigo/violet ground, magenta + cyan neon, gold windows),
  warm interior (apartment lamp: amber), cold monitor light (阿杰's office: blue-white), storm (desaturated violet with
  lightning white), dawn (peach, gold, clearing cobalt). Skin has its own colour in every set.
- **Frame:** full 16:9 (theatrical feature), the tape + CRT pass restrained. Two tape accents: a power-on at the very
  start, and a burst of tracking noise when the drone's scan locks on.
- **Fonts (OFL):** Noto Sans SC Black / Bold for titles, signs and HUD; ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou for the neon signs;
  Barlow Semi Condensed for Latin HUD numerals.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The delivery run (chosen).** One object (the lunchbox) crosses a hostile city at night against the clock: home →
   garage → street → billboard → drone → tower → window → sunrise. A journey structure gives the action cue its spine
   and every obstacle a reason; the lunchbox's steam is a running clock (it must still be hot).
2. **Two screens.** Cross-cut 外婆's TV and 阿杰's monitors through the night until the mech fills his window. Tender,
   but the action becomes something seen on screens; rejected.
3. **The mock opening credits.** The whole film as an anime OP with name cards for each obstacle. Fun but no room for
   the climax to slow down; rejected (its title-card energy survives in the opening).

## 2. Logline and arc

On a typhoon night, a grandmother who once drove construction mechs takes her old yellow machine across a neon
megacity so that her overworked grandson eats a hot meal — and on the 88th floor he finally looks up from the screen.

- **Opening (0–20 s):** tape power-on; a crane down through the rain-soaked neon city to one warm window; title.
- **Setup (20–45 s):** 外婆 at home; the **old photo** (young her in a hard hat, thumbs up, in front of 大黄); the phone
  chat: 「吃了吗？」 →「没空吃」「三天没下楼了」; her face changes; dumplings into the steaming lunchbox, the red cloth
  knot; the garage: the cover flies off 大黄 in a lightning flash; the cockpit; the **dashboard bubble level** (planted);
  the engine coughs, she **smacks the dashboard** (planted), the eyes light.
- **Development (45–100 s):** the drive through traffic (speed lines, light bands); the **billboard** torn off by the
  typhoon falls toward a delivery rider — 大黄 catches it (impact frame) and sets it down; the **security drone** scans
  her as a threat (red HUD, tracking noise) — she holds up the lunchbox; 「识别：饺子」「威胁等级：外婆」; the drone
  turns green and escorts her; the **climb** of the tower in the wind, floor numbers rising, a gust: she keeps the
  lunchbox level by the bubble level (pay-off).
- **Climax (100–118 s):** 88F. 阿杰 at his desk, three days of cans, blue monitor light; tap tap on the glass; he turns;
  a giant yellow hand at the window holding the red-wrapped lunchbox, steaming. **The music drops out**; extreme close-up
  on his eyes; hold; the cockpit lamp: 外婆 waves; he laughs.
- **Ending (118–140 s):** dawn. They eat dumplings on 大黄's palm above the city as the storm clears; the sunrise
  pull-back; 阿杰 takes a phone photo of her giving a thumbs-up in front of 大黄 → **match cut to the old photo**
  (same composition, decades apart); his phone: 「吃了。」 sent to the family chat (pay-off of 「吃了吗？」); end card.

Set-ups → pay-offs: old photo → the new photo (match cut); 「吃了吗？」 → 「吃了。」; the dashboard smack (engine) →
smack again when the drone jams her radio; the bubble level (start-up) → the lunchbox kept level in the gust; the steam
(packing) → steam at the window; the drone's threat HUD → its escort; the rain → the clearing sky.

## 4. Characters & design

- **外婆** (≈ 70): small, round-shouldered, silver hair in a bun with a red hairpin, reading glasses she pushes up into
  her hair when she drives (so the eyes stay readable), a quilted plum jacket, sleeves rolled. Eyes: almond, strong
  brows, smile lines. Expressions: worried (phone), resolute (packing / garage), fierce (driving), sly (drone), tender
  (window, palm). Accent: the red checked cloth.
- **阿杰** (≈ 26): tall and thin, messy black hair, dark circles, an oversized grey hoodie, headphones around the neck,
  lit blue by monitors. Expressions: dull (coding), startled (the tap), stunned (the hand), moved/laughing (the window),
  happy (the palm).
- **大黄**: an old yellow construction mech, boxy, rounded corners, scuffed paint, hazard stripes, a glass cockpit dome in
  the chest, two big eye-lamps, chunky hands with three fingers and a thumb, tracked? no — two stumpy legs; the
  characters 「大黄」 stencilled on the shoulder. Silhouette: a block on short legs with oversized hands.
- **The drone**: a lens-eyed quadcopter with a red/green light bar and a small screen.

## 8. Text design

- Title: chrome gradient 《机甲外婆》 (Noto Sans SC Black, italic-sheared), dark + neon outlines, a small Latin line
  MECHA GRANDMA, flicker-on at 12 fps, light sweep, star glint.
- Phone chat: rounded bubbles (grey incoming, green outgoing), Noto Sans SC Bold.
- HUD and signs: Noto Sans SC Black; neon signs in ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou with emissive glow; generic words only
  (「台风预警」「面馆」「营业中」「大厦」).
- End card: chrome 《机甲外婆》 + 「完」.
