# 《外公的单车》 Grandpa's Bicycle

A watercolour journal kept by a girl, Xiao He, over many years, in the library's Watercolor Brush style (1920×1080, 24 fps, 134 s, Simplified Chinese, no voice-over). Summer, age six: in the lane behind the house her grandpa teaches her to ride his black 28-inch bicycle. She falls three times (「第一次：摔了 3 次」, a painted scraped knee), he holds the back of the saddle, says 「我扶着呢」, and quietly lets go; she rides fifty metres before she looks back and sees him far behind, waving. The pages turn through the years: she rides to school past him at the corner, rides off to the city for university with a bag on the back seat, the bicycle leans unused in the snow while his walking stick appears beside it. Spring: she comes home, lifts him onto the back seat, rings the bell, rides him slowly down the same lane under the blossom and says 「外公，我扶着您呢」. Last page: the two of them small at the end of the lane, 「骑了 50 米，和那年一样」.

**How it was made.** Everything is drawn in code on generated paper: the style's brush-stroke engine (a wash ribbon with dry-brush bristle tracks, no outlines), a 2.5D figure rig with two-bone IK and painted faces (eyes with pupils, lids and brows; blended expressions), a bicycle rig with rotating spokes and crank, and the lane painted twice: in profile for the action and in one-point perspective for the shots about distance. Six journal pages, 33 shots, page turns as the time cuts and paper-coloured mist as the eraser; hand notes in Long Cang, the title and stamp in Noto Serif SC, measured labels in IBM Plex Mono. One timeline (`timeline.js`) carries the sections, tempos and hits, and drives the original score (`score.py`, sampled nylon guitar, clarinet, flute, oboe, strings, felt piano, glockenspiel: a summer waltz, a brisk school page, a minor-leaning autumn, a near-silent winter with two piano notes, a spring that thins to one piano for the close-up) and the procedural foley (`mix.py`: brush, pencil, page turns, bell, chain, falls, steps, cicadas, sparrows, songbirds, wind, a clock, a distant train). Source and `build.sh` are in `films/grandpas-bicycle/` on the branch; `CREDITS` lists every third-party asset (CC0 and CC BY sample libraries, OFL fonts).

**Checks run and results.**
- Cue check (`cuecheck.py`): 20 hits (bell rings, page turns, three falls, the letting-go, the lift, the solo-piano drop, the written line, the last chord); every picture hit and every music cue within 0 ms of the grid.
- Reading time (`core/render/readcheck.mjs`): all 22 on-screen texts hold at least characters ÷ 4.5 + 1.5 s, fully in frame.
- Loudness (two-pass loudnorm in `mux.sh`, measured with ebur128): integrated −14.0 LUFS, LRA 8.1 LU, true peak −1.2 dBTP.
- Black frames (`blackdetect`, d=0.1, pix_th=0.10): none.
- CJK glyph coverage (fontTools against the subset fonts): every character used is present in Noto Serif SC and Long Cang; no fallback boxes on the contact sheets.
- Two full review passes at 1 fps (contact sheets and 0.2 s action strips in `films/grandpas-bicycle/stills/`), with one fix round between them (bell inserts rebuilt, wides brought closer, knee and hand inserts reframed, notes moved off the tree crowns).
- Sign-off grep for the library's credit: nothing found; the last seconds carry only the journal's own stamp.
- Not the demo: structure, opening, signature shot, camera path, score shape and ending all differ from the style's demo film.
