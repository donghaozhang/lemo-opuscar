# 外星人来吃火锅

A 145-second Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon short (1920×1080, 24 fps, Simplified Chinese). On the last rainy night of a tiny Chongqing hot-pot shop, with a 「转让」 sign on the door and three coins on the counter, a pot-lid saucer crash-lands on the roof. Two aliens walk in: tall, deadpan 祖格 and small, nervous 皮皮, whose translator turns "something to fix our ship" into 「最辣的」. Pip's first bite of the nine-grid pot plays out in a cold silence (pupils shrink, red climbs up from his scarf, steam from every vent, then 「再来一盘」). Auntie Liu teaches Zug 七上八下 with chopsticks stuck on his head as antennae. The power cell that flickered near chilli steam on the roof turns out to run on red broth, and they fill it with a ladle. They pay with a glowing hexagon coin set in the gap the third coin rolled out of, and lift off over the night city. Next morning there's a queue round the block, the 「外星人认证」 sign goes up, and a fleet arrives with tiny bowls. Everything was made in code, with no samples, text-to-speech or footage: a Canvas 2D vector engine with boiling constant-width lines renders each frame headless; the voices are formant-synthesised gibberish (low and flat for Zug, high and fast for Pip, a closed-mouth hum for Auntie Liu, beeps for the translator) with lip-sync from their envelopes; the score is synthesised (theremin lead, analog bass, square arpeggio, drum machine, plus bossa, brushed swing, toy piano and tremolo cues), running through tempos of 80/160/120/100/120/60/120 with four hard cuts to silence; all foley is procedural; and the type is ZCOOL KuaiLe (OFL), subset to every character used. Checks on the final file: loudness −14.2 LUFS integrated, true peak −1.1 dBTP (both versions); blackdetect found 0 black frames; the cue check put all 20 visual hits on their beat at 0 ms offset; readcheck passed all 14 on-screen texts; the CJK glyph check found all 99 drawn non-ASCII characters in the font, none missing; two full review passes were done (1 fps contact sheets and 0.2 s key-action strips in `films/alien-hotpot/stills/`), with fixes rendered after each. Files: `final.mp4` (1080p, 61 MB), `final-720p.mp4` (21 MB), `poster.jpg`, `subtitles.srt`. Source, treatment and credits are in `films/alien-hotpot/` (one-command `build.sh`).
