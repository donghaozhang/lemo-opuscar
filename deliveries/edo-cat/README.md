# 江户猫追鱼记 · The Edo Cat and the Fish

A 144.6-second ukiyo-e film (1920×1080, 24 fps, no voice; Chinese on-screen text and `subtitles.srt`): at dawn in Edo a scrawny stray steals one of the fishmonger's **two** fish for the kitten it hides under a bridge, loses it to a black kite, and chases the bird across a handscroll of complete woodblock prints — a bridge in a sudden shower, a snowy temple, rice fields under Fuji, a mountain pass — with the fishmonger suffering one gag behind in each; at the sea the cat rides a fishing boat up a great wave and, in a held silence, leaps *out of the print over its top border* to catch the fish in its teeth; the scroll rolls back home, the dawn print is reprinted as dusk, the kitten eats, and the soaked fishmonger quietly lays the second fish beside them. Everything was made in code for this film (`films/edo-cat/`: `TREATMENT.md`, `build.sh`): a canvas woodblock engine (washi, misregistered colour blocks with woodgrain, baren and specks, per-pixel bokashi, carved key lines, cartouches and seals), code-drawn character rigs with blended poses and an expression set (model and expression sheets in `stills/`), 56 shots cut on a music grid (handscroll slides between views, coverage sheets inside them, tilts, push-ins, a match cut on Fuji, alternate impressions of one key block), an original score for modelled shamisen and koto, reshaped shakuhachi samples, taiko, shime-daiko, hyōshigi and rin (sneaky → silence → accelerating chase → a 5-second silence before the catch → koto lullaby), and synthesised Foley and ambience for paws, wings, fish, rain, snow, bell, waves, wood and paper. Checks run on the final film: two full review passes on 1 fps contact sheets plus 0.2 s action strips (`REVIEW.md`, `stills/review*`); cue check — 16 music hits within 0 ms of their visual hits and all 56 cuts on the beat grid; `readcheck` — every printed text holds long enough (all OK); loudness −14.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −2.7 dBTP (720p: −14.1 LUFS, −1.9 dBTP); `blackdetect` — no black frames; CJK glyph check — 0 missing glyphs in the two OFL subset fonts (Ma Shan Zheng, Noto Serif SC), confirmed on rendered cartouches. Third-party assets and licences are listed in `films/edo-cat/CREDITS` (OFL fonts; CC0 VCSL, VSCO 2 CE and FreePats samples).
